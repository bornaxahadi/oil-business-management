# Oil Business Management — Offer Intake

A mobile web app for collecting oil product offers (crude, fuel oil, diesel, petrol, base oil, condensate, jet fuel, LPG, bitumen) in a structured way. The seller taps through 6 short stages, every question has a **Don't know** option, and at the end the app makes a designed PDF offer sheet.

## How offers reach you
- **Email** — the PDF plus every file the seller attached is emailed to you through [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co) (free, no server).
- **WhatsApp** — the seller can share the PDF straight to WhatsApp from their phone's share menu, or send a text summary to your WhatsApp number.
- **Download** — save the PDF.

## Setup (2 minutes)
1. Open `index.html`, find the `CONFIG` block at the top of the script and set:
   - `receiverEmail` — your email
   - `whatsappNumber` — digits only, e.g. `971501234567`
2. Commit. The live app is on GitHub Pages: `https://bornaxahadi.github.io/oil-business-management/`
3. Send yourself one test offer. FormSubmit emails you a one-time **Activate** link — click it. After that every offer arrives automatically.

Attachments: keep each file under ~5 MB. Large files can be sent on WhatsApp.
